# Appendix: Risk Term Dictionary

This appendix documents the risk term dictionary used for the bag-of-words analysis of Swedish Risk and Vulnerability Analyses (RSA). The dictionary follows a three-tier structure.

## Dictionary Structure

**Tier 1: Individual Risk Terms** — Maps 83 canonical risk concepts to their Swedish linguistic variants (inflections, synonyms, abbreviations). Total: 304 variants.

**Tier 2: Risk Categories** — Groups individual risks into two categories: Security (antagonistic threats) and Other.

**Tier 3: Extended Terms** — Additional filtering terms for risk family detection (not shown in this appendix).

**Sources:**
- MSB Riskkatalog (2025)
- MSB Nationell risk- och sårbarhetsbedömning 2025
- EU Civil Protection Knowledge Network
- EU Global Threats Programme
- EU CBRN Risk Mitigation

## Tier 2: Risk Categories

### Security
Intentional human action including cyber threats, terrorism, military conflict, and crime.

**Risks:** Cyberattack, DDoS attack, ransomware, IT sabotage, terrorism, threats and violence, active shooter, violent extremism, sabotage, espionage, disinformation, hybrid threats, threats to democracy, armed attack, long-range attack, blockade, organized crime, vandalism, human trafficking, drug crime, gang crime

### Other
All non-security risks: natural hazards, technical failures, accidents, infrastructure disruptions, health, economic, and social risks.
